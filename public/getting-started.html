Chronicle Keeper

GM quick start

Your Keeper membership supports one active Foundry world and up to eight players during the beta.

  1. Receive your private world detailsAfter joining, Colesen provisions your Campaign ID and private bridge key. Keep the key private; players never need it.
  2. Install Pocket Chronicle BridgeIn Foundry's Add-on Modules screen, paste the manifest link below. Enable Pocket Chronicle Bridge in your world after Foundry installs it.
  3. Connect your worldOpen Game Settings → Configure Settings → Pocket Chronicle Bridge. Enter the app address, your Campaign ID, and your private bridge key.
  4. Choose your player codeCreate a permanent six-character Campaign code. This is the only connection code your players need; it is not the private bridge key.
  5. Approve each player oncePlayers choose their existing Foundry account. Approve the named request, then they create a separate Pocket Chronicle password.
  6. Run your sessionUse Start Active World and Sync Now during play. End Session creates a readable Sleeping World while live actions, rests, and the shop remain safely closed.

Player setup

Bring your character along

  1. Ask your GM for two thingsYou need the Campaign ID and the permanent six-character Campaign code. You do not need the private bridge key.
  2. Open Pocket ChronicleEnter those two campaign details, then choose your own existing Foundry player account.
  3. Wait for the one-time approvalYour GM approves your named account. Pocket Chronicle will then ask you to create a separate app password.
  4. Choose your characterEvery character assigned to your Foundry account appears automatically. Your GM does not need to pair each sheet separately.
  5. Install it on your phoneOn iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Chrome → menu → Install app or Add to Home screen.
Your safety comes first. Pocket Chronicle will never ask for your Foundry password. The phone receives approved character and journal information, not the Scene canvas. Your Pocket Chronicle password is separate.

A little help

Common fixes

The app says Sleeping World

This is normal outside a live session. Your saved character stays readable, and Pocket Chat remains available. The GM starts Active World when live actions, combat, rests, and shopping should open.

I cannot see my character

Ask the GM to assign the Actor to your Foundry user, then use Sync Now in the Bridge settings. Refresh the phone app afterward.

My password does not work

Tap “Forgot or reset this password,” enter the campaign details again, and ask the GM to approve the named reset request in Bridge settings.

The app looks out of date

Open the three-dot Phone Settings menu and tap Refresh App. Your saved account and Sleeping World data stay in place.
