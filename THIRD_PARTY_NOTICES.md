# Third-party notices

Pocket Chronicle includes `@3d-dice/dice-box` 1.1.4 for its optional, lazy-loaded phone dice animation.

- Project: https://github.com/3d-dice/dice-box
- License: MIT
- Included license: `public/vendor/dice-box/LICENSE.txt`

The renderer is loaded only when a player makes a supported dice roll. Pocket Chronicle's lightweight local roller remains the fallback for reduced-motion preferences, unsupported browsers, large dice pools, and renderer failures.
